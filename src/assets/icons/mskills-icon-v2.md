# MSkills icon concept v2

Preview concept generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The application and installer continue using their existing icon while the design is being refined.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original premium macOS app icon for MSkills, a skill library and management tool. Produce ONE final square icon, not a presentation board. Artistic direction: highly restrained, architectural, editorial, precise. A smooth warm ivory macOS squircle tile with a very subtle edge and slight lift, on a genuinely transparent canvas outside the tile. In the center, ONE bold charcoal geometric emblem: a distinctive abstract M-like mark made from two interlocking folded modules, suggesting organized reusable skill components. The emblem should look like a carefully constructed symbol, not a typed letter: two solid angled sections, coherent proportions, a clean central negative-space notch, subtly softened outer corners and precise inner cuts. A flat graphic silhouette, almost entirely black and off-white, large enough to read clearly at 24px. Plenty of calm negative space around the emblem. Exceptional optical balance and finish, quiet professional productivity software identity. No decorative star or sparkle, no overlapping card illustration, no gradients on the emblem, no glossy plastic, no chrome, no neon, no bright blue or purple, no texture, no AI robot or brain, no written app name, no typography, no extra symbols, no surrounding scenery or mockup, no multiple variants. Keep it sophisticated and simple rather than visually busy. Transparent exterior with clean alpha and no stray marks.

## Edge refinement prompt

Precise cleanup of this MSkills app icon. Keep the two charcoal geometric M modules exactly the same shape, size, position and optical balance. Keep the warm off-white square tile and its rounded-square geometry. Remove all ragged, grainy, irregular and dirty fringe outside the tile, especially along the top and side edges. Rebuild the tile boundary as a perfectly smooth, clean, sharp antialiased rounded-square contour, like precise vector artwork. Make the tile surface flatter and more restrained, remove the bulky beveled frame and drop shadow. Outside that precise contour must be genuinely transparent with no stray pixels or noise. Preserve the black-and-ivory palette and original central symbol. Clean final production app icon, no text and no additional elements.
